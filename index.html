How it works

Three steps. One script tag.

You paste the script. You pick a backend. You see your data. We are completely uninvolved after step 1.

1

Paste the script tag

One line in your site's head. Done. It starts tracking immediately.

2

Configure your endpoint

Supabase, Airtable, Google Sheets, or any URL that accepts a POST request. Your data, your database, your rules.

3

See your data in your own dashboard

Open your Supabase table, your Airtable base, your spreadsheet. It's all there. We never touched it.

Your data goes directly to your backend. We literally cannot see it. We don't have servers.
index.html
<!-- Paste this in your <head> --> <script src="https://henryratterman.com/poorjar/poorjar.js" data-endpoint="https://your-endpoint.supabase.co/..." data-site-id="your-site-id" async ></script> // That's it. It starts tracking: // clicks, scrolls, rage clicks, mouse movement. // Batched and sent to your endpoint every 30s.
What PoorJar tracks

The stuff that actually matters

Click heatmaps
Where people actually click vs where you think they click. Usually not the same thing.
Scroll maps
Find out nobody reads past your hero section. It will hurt. You will learn.
Rage clicks
The button that's making people lose their minds. 3 rapid clicks = someone is frustrated. Fix it.
Mouse movement
Watch people hover nervously over your pricing. Fascinating. Also a little sad.
Session replay coming soon
Watch real sessions. It's humbling. We're building it. Probably.
What PoorJar doesn't do

The part where we brag about what's missing

Sometimes the best feature is the one you didn't build.

✕ Send your data to our servers. We don't have any. This is load-bearing.
✕ Charge you $99/month. Or $49/month. Or any amount per month.
✕ Send you a cold email sequence with subject line "just checking in..."
✕ Ask you to hop on a quick call with our enterprise team.
✕ Have a Starter, Growth, Scale, Business, and Enterprise tier where each tier removes one arbitrary limitation.
✕ Require you to create an account, verify your email, fill out "what brings you to PoorJar today?", and sit through an onboarding checklist.
