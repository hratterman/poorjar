FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is this actually useful? +
Our customers say yes. We choose to believe them. The case studies are available upon request but we recommend scheduling a 30-minute discovery call first.
What does "AI-native" mean? +
It means we use GPT-4 somewhere in the product. Our engineering team is still figuring out exactly where. The important thing is we put it in the marketing copy before we figured out the product. Standard operating procedure.
Why is it called Deliverabls? +
The domain with the e was taken. We considered Deliverables.io, Deliverables.co, Deliverables.app, and Deliverables.xyz. All taken. We briefly considered pivoting the product to fit a different name. This was faster.
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely. Probably. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant (we think). We take security very seriously, as evidenced by the fact that we're mentioning it in the FAQ rather than burying it in a policy document nobody reads.
How quickly can we get onboarded? +
Most teams are fully onboarded in 2-4 weeks. We'll schedule an onboarding kickoff, a platform walkthrough, a stakeholder alignment session, a change management workshop, and a go-live ceremony. We find that teams appreciate the ceremony.
